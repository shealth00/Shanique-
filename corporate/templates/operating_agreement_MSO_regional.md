## RECITALS

This Operating Agreement ("Agreement") governs MSO-Regional Entities LLC (the "Company"), a limited liability company formed to provide management and administrative services to healthcare providers across multiple states. Each state in which the Company operates has distinct regulatory requirements for management services organizations, and this Agreement shall be interpreted and applied in a manner consistent with applicable law in each operating jurisdiction.

## ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE OF SERVICES

**2.1 General Purpose.** To provide comprehensive management and administrative services to healthcare providers and practices operating across multiple states.

**2.2 Services.** The Company's services shall include, but are not limited to:

(a) Multi-state administrative and operational management;
(b) Revenue cycle management, coding, and billing services;
(c) Multi-state payer credentialing and contracting;
(d) Human resources, benefits administration, and workforce management;
(e) Information technology, EHR implementation, and data management;
(f) Regulatory compliance programs (HIPAA, OSHA, state-specific);
(g) Quality assurance and performance improvement programs;
(h) Training, education, and professional development programs;
(i) Supply chain management and group purchasing;
(j) Marketing, branding, and patient engagement services; and
(k) Real estate, lease negotiation, and facility management.

**2.3 Corporate Practice Prohibition.** The Company shall not engage in the corporate practice of medicine, nursing, dentistry, psychology, or any other licensed healthcare profession in any operating state. All clinical decisions and professional judgments shall remain exclusively with licensed healthcare professionals. Management Services Agreements shall be structured to comply with each state's corporate practice doctrine.

**2.4 State-Specific Service Limitations.** Where a particular state restricts or prohibits specific management services activities, the Company's Management Services Agreements in that state shall be modified accordingly. A state-specific addendum shall be attached to each Management Services Agreement identifying jurisdiction-specific limitations.

## ARTICLE 4 — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**4.1 Allocation.** Profits and losses shall be allocated among Members in proportion to their Membership Interests, except as otherwise required by the Internal Revenue Code or Treasury Regulations.

**4.2 Distributions.** Distributions shall be made as determined by the Managers, in proportion to Membership Interests, subject to maintenance of adequate reserves for multi-state operational requirements.

**4.3 State Tax Withholding.** The Company shall comply with all state income tax withholding requirements in each operating state, including composite returns, withholding on non-resident Members, and state-specific pass-through entity taxes.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — MANAGEMENT

**5.1 Manager-Managed.** The Company shall be managed by one or more Managers as identified on **Exhibit B**.

**5.2 Regional Directors.** The Managers may appoint Regional Directors for each operating state or region, with authority to manage day-to-day operations within that jurisdiction, subject to the overall supervision of the Managers.

**5.3 Actions Requiring Member Approval.**

The following shall require affirmative vote of Members holding at least ___% of Units:

(a) Entering a new state or ceasing operations in an existing state;
(b) Executing or terminating a Management Services Agreement with annual contract value exceeding $_______________;
(c) Incurring debt or obligations in excess of $_______________;
(d) Acquiring or disposing of real property;
(e) Hiring or terminating executive-level employees;
(f) Amending this Agreement;
(g) Dissolving the Company; and
(h) Any merger, acquisition, joint venture, or strategic alliance.

**5.4 Multi-State Compliance Officer.** The Company shall designate a Chief Compliance Officer (or equivalent) responsible for monitoring regulatory requirements in each operating state, maintaining the state compliance calendar, and ensuring that Management Services Agreements comply with applicable law.

## ARTICLE 6 — STATE COMPLIANCE SCHEDULE

**6.1 Compliance Calendar.** The Managers shall maintain a compliance calendar tracking annual report deadlines, registered agent renewals, and Certificates of Good Standing refresh requirements for each operating state. See **Exhibit C** for the current State Registration Schedule.

**6.2 Annual Reports.** The Company shall file all required annual reports, biennial reports, or equivalent filings in each operating state by the applicable deadline.

**6.3 Good Standing.** The Company shall maintain its good standing in all operating states. Certificates of Good Standing shall be obtained from each state's Secretary of State office within 90 days of any financing event, major contract execution, or licensing application.

**6.4 Registered Agents.** The Company shall maintain a registered agent with a physical address in each operating state. Changes to registered agents shall be filed promptly with the applicable Secretary of State.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENTS

**7.1 Required Provisions.** All Management Services Agreements entered by the Company shall:

(a) Clearly delineate non-clinical (administrative) services from clinical services;
(b) Establish a fair market value management fee supported by an independent valuation or documented comparable analysis;
(c) Include a termination clause with reasonable notice periods;
(d) Contain HIPAA Business Associate Agreement provisions where applicable;
(e) Include state-specific addenda addressing jurisdiction-specific regulatory requirements;
(f) Be reviewed and approved by qualified healthcare regulatory counsel prior to execution; and
(g) Include anti-kickback and Stark Law compliance representations.

**7.2 Fee Structures.** Management fees shall be set at fair market value and shall not be structured as a percentage of revenue, a percentage of collections, or any other arrangement that could constitute unlawful fee-splitting or a financial relationship under applicable anti-kickback or Stark Law provisions, unless a specific exception applies and has been confirmed by legal counsel.
