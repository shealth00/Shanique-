## ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

**2.1 General Purpose.** The Company is organized for any lawful purpose permitted under the Texas BOC.

**2.2 MSO Services.** Without limitation, the Company is organized to provide management and administrative services to healthcare providers and practices operating in the State of Texas, including:

(a) Administrative and operational management services;
(b) Revenue cycle management and billing services;
(c) Human resources, staffing, and payroll administration;
(d) Credentialing, payer contracting, and managed care support;
(e) Information technology infrastructure and EHR system management;
(f) Regulatory compliance and quality assurance support;
(g) Real property management and facility services; and
(h) Such other non-clinical services as the Members may determine from time to time.

**2.3 Corporate Practice Prohibition.** The Company shall not engage in the corporate practice of medicine, dentistry, or any other licensed healthcare profession. All clinical decisions shall remain exclusively with duly licensed healthcare professionals. All Management Services Agreements shall be reviewed by qualified healthcare regulatory counsel prior to execution.

## ARTICLE 4 — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**4.1 Profits and Losses.** Except as otherwise required by law, profits and losses of the Company for each fiscal year shall be allocated among the Members in proportion to their respective Membership Interests (as set forth on Exhibit A).

**4.2 Distributions.** Distributions shall be made to the Members at such times and in such amounts as determined by the Managers, in proportion to their respective Membership Interests, subject to any reserves deemed reasonably necessary by the Managers.

**4.3 Withholding.** The Company may withhold distributions to pay taxes, fees, or other amounts owed by a Member with respect to the Member's interest in the Company.

**4.4 Limitations.** No distribution shall be made to any Member if, after giving effect to the distribution, the Company would be unable to pay its debts as they become due in the ordinary course of business.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — MANAGEMENT

**5.1 Manager-Managed.** The Company shall be managed by one or more Managers. The initial Manager(s) are identified on **Exhibit B**.

**5.2 Authority of Managers.** Subject to the limitations set forth in this Agreement, the Managers shall have full authority to manage and control the business and affairs of the Company.

**5.3 Actions Requiring Member Approval.** The following actions shall require the affirmative vote or written consent of Members holding at least ___% of the outstanding Units:

(a) Amending this Operating Agreement or the Certificate of Formation;
(b) Admitting new Members;
(c) Merging, consolidating, or converting the Company;
(d) Selling, leasing, or disposing of all or substantially all of the Company's assets outside the ordinary course of business;
(e) Dissolving the Company;
(f) Making distributions other than in proportion to Membership Interests;
(g) Incurring indebtedness in excess of $_______________; and
(h) Entering into any Management Services Agreement or amending any existing Management Services Agreement.

**5.4 Officers.** The Managers may designate officers of the Company with such titles, duties, and authority as the Managers may determine.

**5.5 Compensation.** Managers and officers may receive reasonable compensation for services rendered to the Company, as determined by the Members.

**5.6 Standard of Care.** Each Manager shall act in good faith, in a manner the Manager reasonably believes to be in the best interests of the Company.

## ARTICLE 7 — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTERESTS

**7.1 Restrictions on Transfer.** No Member may transfer all or any portion of such Member's Membership Interest without the prior written consent of the Managers and Members holding a majority of Units, which consent may be withheld in their sole discretion.

**7.2 Right of First Refusal.** Prior to any permitted transfer to a third party, the selling Member shall offer the interest to the remaining Members on a pro-rata basis.

**7.3 Permitted Transfers.** Notwithstanding the foregoing, a Member may transfer a Membership Interest to: (a) a wholly-owned entity controlled by that Member; or (b) a trust for the benefit of the Member or the Member's immediate family, provided that the Member retains voting control.

**7.4 Healthcare Regulatory Compliance.** Any transfer of a Membership Interest shall be conditioned upon receipt of a legal opinion from qualified healthcare regulatory counsel confirming that such transfer does not violate applicable federal or state healthcare laws, including without limitation the Anti-Kickback Statute and any applicable state fee-splitting prohibitions.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — WITHDRAWAL AND DISSOLUTION

**8.1 Withdrawal.** A Member may withdraw from the Company only upon [___ days'] prior written notice and only with the unanimous written consent of the remaining Members.

**8.2 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon:

(a) The unanimous written consent of all Members;
(b) Entry of a judicial decree of dissolution; or
(c) As otherwise required by the Texas BOC.

**8.3 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Managers (or a liquidating trustee if no Manager is then serving) shall wind up the Company's affairs, pay all debts and liabilities, and distribute remaining assets to the Members in proportion to their Membership Interests.

## ARTICLE 9 — INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

**9.1 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless each Member and Manager from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of the business of the Company or any Member's or Manager's actions on behalf of the Company, except to the extent arising from gross negligence, fraud, or willful misconduct.

**9.2 Limitation of Liability.** No Member or Manager shall be personally liable for any debt, obligation, or liability of the Company solely by reason of being a Member or Manager.
