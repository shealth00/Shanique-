## ARTICLE VI — PURPOSE

**6.1 General Purpose**
The purpose of this corporation is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which corporations may be organized under the Texas Business Organizations Code.

**6.2 Specific Purpose**
Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the corporation is organized to provide management and administrative services to healthcare providers, including but not limited to:

- Practice management and administrative services
- Billing and revenue cycle management
- Human resources and staffing support
- Credentialing and payer contracting
- Information technology and EHR management
- Compliance and regulatory support
- Real estate and facility management services

> **IMPORTANT — MSO COMPLIANCE NOTE:** The corporation shall not engage in the corporate practice of medicine. All clinical decisions shall remain with licensed healthcare professionals. Any management services agreement between the corporation and a healthcare practice must be reviewed by qualified healthcare regulatory counsel to ensure compliance with Texas law and applicable federal regulations, including the Anti-Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b(b)) and the Stark Law (42 U.S.C. § 1395nn).

## ARTICLE VII — LIABILITY OF DIRECTORS

To the fullest extent permitted by the Texas Business Organizations Code, as it exists or may hereafter be amended, a director of the corporation shall not be liable to the corporation or its shareholders for monetary damages for an act or omission in the director's capacity as a director, except to the extent otherwise expressly provided by applicable statute.

## ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION
